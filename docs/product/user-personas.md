# User personas

**Status:** Active hypotheses  
**Owner:** Product design  
**Reference when:** Designing flows, copy, testing, and prioritisation.  
**Agent obligation:** Use these as testable contexts, not stereotypes.

## Primary: player

**Example:** Mei, 68.  
**Range:** Adult aged 60-75; may be active, occasionally active, or new to
structured exercise.  
**Job:** "Give me a fun, understandable activity that does not make me feel
tested."

Needs:

- a successful first movement quickly;
- clear adult instruction;
- safe standing and seated hand/finger choices;
- control of camera, data, and supporters;
- progress without a health grade.

Do not assume low literacy, poor technology skill, frailty, stubbornness, or a
specific relationship with family.

## Secondary: supporter

**Example:** Jun, 39, player-approved family member or caregiver.  
**Job:** "Tell me when a friendly check-in may be useful without asking me to
interpret medical data."

Needs:

- explicit sharing scope;
- a short change explanation;
- data-quality and uncertainty context;
- editable, calm communication;
- clear access-ended state after revocation.

## Tertiary: facilitator

**Example:** Lin, 57, community dance organiser.  
**Job:** "Help someone start safely and keep the social activity flowing."

The facilitator is not automatically a supporter and receives no data without a
grant.

## Judge/demo viewer

Needs:

- understandable value in under 30 seconds;
- visible AI contribution;
- a reliable live interaction;
- honest simulation and safety boundaries.

Judge convenience never overrides player consent or truthful claims.

## Research/clinical adviser

Future reviewer of task safety and evidence. Not an MVP end user and does not
receive a clinical dashboard.
