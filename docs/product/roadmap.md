**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Product lead  
**Last reviewed:** 26 July 2026  
**Reference when:** Selecting work or changing scope.  
**Agent obligation:** Work in milestone order and apply the scope freeze.

**Exit:**

  cross-linked.
- Documentation inventory and links validate.

**Target:** 26-27 July

- Select and measure hand landmarks for seated gestures.

evidence.

**Target:** 2-3 August

- Clearly labelled simulated trend history.
- Separate supporter grant and revocation.
- Real owner-test check-in through the selected WeChat test path.

**Exit:** Consent-to-send-to-revoke journey passes; no medical claims.

## M4 - Product polish

**Target:** 4-6 August

- Miora asset integration and provenance.
- Simplified Chinese primary copy; English judge toggle.
- Accessibility and reduced motion.
- Performance, fallback, permission, lighting, and multi-body states.
- Formative tests with adults 60-75 and supporters.

## M5 - Submission

**Target:** 7-9 August, subject to portal confirmation

- Five consecutive live demo runs.
- Signed-out production web smoke.
- Demo video and project deck.
- AI evidence pack.
- Rights and privacy review.
- Submission receipt retained.

## Scope freeze

After 3 August, true multiplayer, arbitrary-song mode, new environments,
production health alerts, clinician surfaces, and unrelated cloud work remain
out unless all Must acceptance and release gates already pass.
