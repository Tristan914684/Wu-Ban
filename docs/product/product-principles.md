# Product principles

**Status:** Binding  
**Owner:** Product lead  
**Reference when:** Evaluating scope, copy, UX, metrics, or a trade-off.  
**Agent obligation:** Reject work that conflicts with these principles unless
the product owner records a superseding decision.

1. **Joy before measurement.** The game must be worth playing without the trend
   layer.
2. **Dignity over fear.** No shame, infantilisation, diagnosis theatre, or
   alarming health language.
3. **Personal pattern over population ranking.** Compare a player with their
   own valid baseline.
4. **Consent is a feature.** Disclosure, purpose, recipient, revocation, and
   deletion are part of the core flow.
5. **Safety beats choreography.** Standing movements are bounded; seated
   hand/finger play is a real alternative.
6. **Separate meanings.** Fun score, session measures, and trends never collapse
   into one number.
7. **Uncertainty is visible.** Tracking, fatigue, pain, environment, and
   learning effects are not hidden.
8. **AI is specific.** Pose inference and Miora/CodeBuddy use are demonstrated;
   deterministic rules remain described as rules.
9. **One polished proof beats five incomplete promises.** Protect the
   four-minute loop and demo reliability.
10. **Evidence changes claims.** Marketing language cannot outrun validation.

## Quality bar

A feature is product-ready for the hackathon only if:

- its user job is obvious;
- it works in the main and recovery path;
- consent and safety impacts are addressed;
- it contributes to Impact, AI Use, or Project Quality;
- it can be demonstrated reliably;
- its simulated or sandbox status is unmistakable;
- it does not add a new unowned operational burden.
