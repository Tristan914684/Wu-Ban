# Content design

**Status:** Binding  
**Owner:** Product design  
**Reference when:** Writing UI, consent, errors, trends, notifications, deck, or
localization.  
**Agent obligation:** Use adult, plain, non-diagnostic language and preserve
meaning across Simplified Chinese and English.

## Voice

Warm, direct, respectful, and specific. Never childish, clinical, patronising,
or falsely certain.

## Sentence rules

- Lead with what happened.
- Give one next action.
- Name uncertainty where it matters.
- Prefer common verbs.
- Avoid unexplained abbreviations.
- Keep instructions positive: "Step inside the outline," not "You are out of
  bounds."

## Canonical language

Use:

- "movement-and-attention trend";
- "usual pattern";
- "clear session";
- "check-in";
- "video stays on this device";
- "this is not a diagnosis."

Do not use:

- "cognitive profile";
- "brain age";
- "MCI detected";
- "high risk";
- "decline confirmed";
- "patient";
- "failed session."

## Error pattern

`What happened` + `why it matters if useful` + `one recovery`.

> We lost sight of your hands, so this part will not affect your pattern. Move
> into the outline to continue.

## Localization

- Simplified Chinese is the primary player language.
- English supports judges and development.
- Do not concatenate translated fragments.
- Keep message IDs semantic.
- Review safety and consent copy with a fluent human.
- Record dialect support as separate audio/content variants.
